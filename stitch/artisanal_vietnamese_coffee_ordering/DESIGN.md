## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a refined digital flagship for artisanal Vietnamese coffee culture. Drawing from modern editorial minimalism intertwined with tactile, warm hospitality, the interface marries the timeless ritual of phin brewing with friction-free modern digital commerce.

### Brand Personality & Core Tenets
- **Artisanal & Sophisticated:** Evoking the aroma of slow-dripped Robusta, dark roast blends, and warm condensed milk without clutter or cliché. High-contrast typography paired with measured negative space conveys curation and culinary precision.
- **Warm & Welcoming:** Grounded in Vietnamese cafe culture (*cà phê la cà*), the emotional tone is hospitable, sensory, and approachable rather than cold or aloof.
- **Convenient & Trustworthy:** Streamlined ordering, transparent order progress, and seamless cashless checkouts evoke certainty and speed during peak morning and midday rush hours.

### Visual Style Movement
The design blends **Warm Editorial Minimalism** with soft **Tactile Depth**. It uses crisp, high-contrast serif headers paired with clear, humanist body copy, placed atop creamy, textured backgrounds. Surfaces rely on warm neutral borders, subtle elevation steps, and carefully balanced inner margins to evoke tactile menus, packaging labels, and contemporary boutique cafe interiors.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the roasting spectrum, slow phin extractions, and natural cream pairings. The default display mode is light, capturing an open, sunlit cafe ambience.

### Palette Roles & Application

- **Primary (`#4B2E2B` - Deep Espresso):** The core anchor. Used for primary call-to-actions, prominent headers, navigation accents, active states, and dominant visual weights.
- **Secondary (`#6F4E37` - Roast Brown):** Supporting structural color. Applied to category headers, sub-navigation tabs, step indicators, and interactive borders.
- **Tertiary (`#C58B55` - Warm Caramel):** Used selectively for highlights, promotion badges, loyalty point rewards, order confirmation cues, and key conversion accents.
- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base Background: `#FAF7F2` (Pure Warm Cream) creates an organic canvas softer than harsh sterile white.
  - Card & Container Fill: `#FFFFFF` (Surface White) provides pristine contrast for drink imagery and product modifiers.
  - Muted Inset / Subtle Fill: `#EFE6DA` (Soft Beige) provides structure for disabled fields, nested card groupings, and item counter controls.
- **Typographic Contrast:**
  - Primary Text: `#27211E` (Dark Roast Charcoal) ensures WCAG AAA accessibility across light surfaces.
  - Secondary Text: `#786F69` (Subtle Earth) reserved for metadata, descriptions, pricing comparisons, and phin brew origin tags.
  - Border & Dividers: `#E8DFD5` creates clean structural separation without high-contrast visual noise.
- **Feedback & Semantics:**
  - Success: `#2E7D5B` (Botanical Green) for payment complete, loyalty verified, and customized additions.
  - Warning: `#D99836` (Golden Toast) for low stock, peak queue wait-times, and table transfer alerts.
  - Error: `#C84C4C` (Red Ochre) for payment failures, validation errors, and item stock-outs.

## Typography

Typography balances heritage and practical mobile clarity. All diacritics and complex tone marks inherent to Vietnamese receive ample vertical clearance via relaxed line heights.

### Editorial Headings (`Noto Serif`)
- Reserved for page titles, category headers (e.g., *Cà Phê Phin Truyền Thống*, *Trà & Tráng Miệng*), promotional banners, and receipt headlines.
- Adds an editorial, curated aura reminiscent of high-end culinary publications and historical Vietnamese coffee salons.

### Functional Body & Labels (`Be Vietnam Pro`)
- Designed natively for the Vietnamese writing system, preventing tone clip issues and uneven vowel rendering.
- Applied across all interactive elements, customization controls (ice levels, sweetness options, milk choices), cart tallies, and transactional checkout flows.
- Maintains strict legibility at dense mobile viewports with weights spanning Regular (400) for descriptions, Medium (500) for chips and buttons, and Semi-Bold (600) for item titles and pricing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt baseline rhythm with responsive adaptation targeted at mobile-first in-store table QR scanning, as well as desktop pre-ordering.

### Grid & Viewport Adaptation
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column fluid feed with a fixed sticky bottom cart/action drawer. Canvas padding uses `margin-mobile` (16px), with `gutter-mobile` (12px) separating menu item cards in two-column grid sections.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout. Split layout displays categorized menus on the left 5 columns, with persistent checkout orders on the right 3 columns.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** Max container width bounded at 1200px centered. 12-column layout with 24px gutters and 32px canvas margins. Category anchor navigation fixes to the left side, menu grids occupy the central region, and active cart/receipt summaries dock to the right.

### Vertical Rhythm
Section transitions use `space-xl` (40px) to give menu categories breathing room. Card internals preserve a minimum of `space-md` (16px) padding to prevent tap target crowding around quantity adjusters and customization pills.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is conveyed through delicate, warm-tinted ambient shadows and structured surface borders rather than abrupt heavy drops. Shadows are tinted with `#4B2E2B` at low opacities to echo warm ambient cafe lighting.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Applied to the base canvas (`#FAF7F2`) and inactive option containers. Completely flat, bounded by a 1px solid `#E8DFD5` border.
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards):** Applied to product cards, drink item listings, and modifier groups. Features a white background with a subtle ambient tint: `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(75, 46, 43, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(75, 46, 43, 0.03)` with a 1px border in `#E8DFD5`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Surfaces):** Used when hovering cards or tapping selectable add-on options. Shadow deepens: `box-shadow: 0 8px 20px -4px rgba(75, 46, 43, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(75, 46, 43, 0.04)`. The border shifts to `#C58B55` or `#4B2E2B`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Bars & Bottom Drawers):** Reserved for sticky mobile checkout trays, floating order progress pill badges, and option customization sheets: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -6px rgba(75, 46, 43, 0.12), 0 4px 12px 0 rgba(75, 46, 43, 0.06)`.
- **Level 4 (Modal Dialogs & Payment Confirmations):** Overlays with high visual focus, centered over a 40% opacity espresso-toned backdrop blur: `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(39, 33, 30, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary uses gentle, organic geometry (level 2 roundedness), mirroring the smoothed rim of ceramic phin cups and custom cafe glassware.

### Curvature Scale
- **Base Components (`rounded`, 8px / 0.5rem):** Form inputs, modifier choice tiles, counter increment buttons, and table QR code cards.
- **Mid-Tier Elements (`rounded-lg`, 16px / 1rem):** Coffee card containers, loyalty stamp cards, modal confirmation boxes, and banner displays.
- **Containers & Drawers (`rounded-xl`, 24px / 1.5rem):** Floating bottom checkout bars, sliding product modifier sheets, and hero promotional showcases.
- **Pills (`rounded-full`):** Filter tags, sugar/ice selection chips, status badges (e.g., *Đang pha chế*, *Đã thanh toán*), and floating action triggers.

## Components

All components are tailored for Vietnamese language strings, ensuring sufficient horizontal padding for compound words (e.g., *Thanh toán ngay*, *Tùy chỉnh món*).

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Thanh toán / Thêm vào giỏ):** Background `#4B2E2B`, text `#FFFFFF`, rounded 8px (`rounded`), font weight Semi-Bold (600). Hover shifts background to `#6F4E37`. Minimum height 48px for effortless thumb tap targets.
- **Secondary Action (Xem lại đơn / Chọn lại):** Background `#EFE6DA`, text `#4B2E2B`, border 1px solid `#E8DFD5`. Hover transitions background to `#E8DFD5`.
- **Accent Action (Áp dụng ưu đãi / Tích điểm):** Background `#C58B55`, text `#FFFFFF`. Used for high-value transactional perks.

### Chips & Attribute Selectors (Mức đường, Mức đá, Loại sữa)
- **Unselected:** Border 1px solid `#E8DFD5`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#786F69`. Shape is pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) or soft rectangle (`rounded`).
- **Selected:** Border 1.5px solid `#4B2E2B`, background `#FAF7F2`, text `#4B2E2B`, font weight 600. Accompanied by a subtle caramel checkmark indicator or bold ring highlight.

### Coffee Menu Cards
- Elevated at Level 1, framed by a 1px `#E8DFD5` border, rounded 16px (`rounded-lg`).
- High-contrast layout: Crisp square or 4:3 product photography on top or left, followed by the item title in `title-md` (`Be Vietnam Pro`), origin/blend note in `body-sm` (`#786F69`), and currency-formatted price (e.g., `45.000 ₫`) anchored in `title-md` `#4B2E2B`.
- Integrated quick-add button (`+`) positioned at the bottom right corner in `#4B2E2B` with inverse white icon.

### Form Inputs & Voucher Fields
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1.5px solid `#E8DFD5`, border-radius 8px (`rounded`), text `#27211E`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#4B2E2B` with a 2px outer aura ring in `rgba(197, 139, 85, 0.25)`. Error state uses `#C84C4C` border and helper text.
- Text placeholders rendered in `#786F69` (e.g., *Nhập mã giảm giá...*, *Ghi chú cho quán...*).

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls (Topping & Milk Add-ons)
- 20px x 20px control footprint with 8px clearance.
- Checked state fills with `#4B2E2B` carrying a pure white icon mark.
- Unchecked state features an inset white background with 1.5px `#E8DFD5` outline.

### Quantity Stepper Controls
- Encased pill or compact box in `#EFE6DA`.
- Plus/minus buttons in `#4B2E2B` (hovering to `#6F4E37`), sandwiching a center numerical count in `title-sm` `#27211E`.

### Order Status & Payment Badge (Trạng thái đơn hàng)
- Compact pill element (`rounded-full`) with an 8px colored dot and `label-sm` copy.
- Unpaid/Pending: `#FAF7F2` background, `#D99836` border and text (*Chờ thanh toán*).
- Processing/Brewing: `#FAF7F2` background, `#C58B55` border and text (*Đang pha chế*).
- Ready/Delivered: `#FAF7F2` background, `#2E7D5B` border and text (*Mời nhận món*).
